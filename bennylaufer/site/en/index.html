About Benny

Musician, arranger and conductor

Benny Laufer is an Israeli musician, arranger, composer and conductor, and the son of the music legend Moshe Laufer.

The orchestra carries his name, and he conducts it in every production. Every event begins with a conversation about what matters to you and your family's customs, and from there we build the musical program together.
