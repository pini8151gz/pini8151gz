# BRIEF: Drive Cafe | דרייב קפה

> Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The client set the direction, data, palette and structure in their own words (quoted below). Where they were silent, the decisions are authored and labelled.

## 1. The eight topics

1. **Vibe (client):** "טון אופטימי, חם ומזמין, כמו עצירה מרעננת באמצע הדרך". "קל להגיע, קשה להפסיק ליהנות".
2. **Journey (client):** layered hero (mountains, food trucks, sunset light), then the three trucks with a cinematic move between them, the tabun, outdoor seating and atmosphere, then hours and contact.
3. **Energy (authored):** calm arrival, a quicker middle (the trucks), a quiet beat, then the loudest moment (the tabun), then easing down to a warm, resolved close.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment (client):** "Emotional peak אחד מתוכנן: הגעה לנוף או יציאת הפיצה מהטאבון". Chosen: the pizza coming out of the tabun.
5. **What no other site does (client seed):** "גלילה שמפעילה את הטאבון". Developed into the signature move below.
6. **Aesthetic family (client):** "Restrained motion + premium typography". Warm and light, premium but not minimal: food is abundant.
7. **One world or scenes (authored):** distinct scenes. It is a stop on a road, not a continuous flight.
8. **Assets (client):** 84 real photos from @drive_cafe (`drive-cafe/assets/instagram`). Nothing generated. No video.

## 5. The peak

> "You scroll and the tabun fires up, and the pizza comes out of the oven right at you."

Lives in act 5. Largest span on the page (3.6 viewports). Preceded by the quiet act 4.

**It's the site where:** you scroll and the pizza comes out of the tabun at you.

## 6. Structure

- **Grammar:** Filmic one-shot. The client named its devices word for word: pinned sections, headings built line by line, ground that shifts with scroll, a layered hero. Chaptered editorial (no media hero) and typographic poster (no photography) contradict a 84-photo food brand; gallery/catalog bans the kinetic headlines asked for; split stage has no two-sided argument; live surface is for software; continuous world needs one geography and video; rhythmic cutlist bans pin, which the client requested.
- **Signature move: "The firing."** A bespoke act driven from `--sc-p` in page code: the oven photo sits in darkness, a heat glow and rising embers grow with scroll, the flames brighten, then the pizza (real alpha cut from their photo) travels out of the oven mouth toward the viewer, growing and tilting flat, and settles as the frame fills. Ember particles are a canvas that reacts to scroll velocity. Not a kit device.
- **Authored silence:** act 4 is intentionally sparse. It is not dead scroll.

## 7. Score

| # | Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrival | layered `parallax` (6 planes), not pinned | depth hero required; no video exists, so planes carry it. Not pinned so it never sits beside act 2's pin |
| 2 | Recognition | `pin` + `kinetic` | lines of the morning assemble over the junction sign |
| 3 | Choice | `pan` | three trucks are a range, lateral reads as options |
| 4 | Trust | `flow` + `in` | quiet document beat, contrast before the peak |
| 5 | Awe (peak) | bespoke pin: "the firing" | the signature move |
| 6 | Appetite | `reveal` + tabs | wipes are a change of state; the menu is useful |
| 7 | Belonging | `flow` + iris `reveal` | the view opens |
| 8 | Resolve | `pin` + `magnet` + live status | the page stops moving and answers |

Families: parallax, pin/kinetic, pan, flow, bespoke, reveal, pointer. Seven. No device family repeats back to back.

## 8. Mobile

Separate composition: hero bus moves to lower third and the headline sits above it (not between planes), shake foreground smaller and to one corner, pan panels full width, tabun crop centred on the mouth, menu tabs become a horizontal scroller, close stacks.
